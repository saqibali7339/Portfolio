AI Visibility Tool

A clean, practical dashboard for tracking prompt-level AI answer presence, citation visibility, and source-page signals

This tool is designed to help brands, publishers, SEO teams, and site owners monitor how they appear across AI-powered answers. Instead of only checking rankings in traditional search, it focuses on whether a brand is mentioned directly in AI answers, referenced in citations, supported by source-page visibility, and present across valuable prompt opportunities.

The dashboard works best as an active monitoring layer. Results are based on live AI-answer analysis, citation tracking, and referenced source-page signals, so visibility can evolve over time as prompts, sources, and AI systems update. That makes the tool especially useful for spotting opportunities, comparing prompts, and improving the pages that deserve the most attention.

How to read these insights: The dashboard is built for dynamic AI visibility monitoring. It is best used to compare prompt opportunities, surface strong and weak visibility patterns, and guide SEO actions such as improving entity clarity, building citation-worthy pages, and expanding prompt-focused content.

What this tool tracks

Monitor AI answer presence, citation visibility, source-page signals, prompt-level performance, and overall visibility score in one workflow.

What you can do with it

  • Create projects for different brands or websites
  • Track prompt sets for commercial, informational, or brand queries
  • Measure AI answer presence and citation presence separately
  • Review prompt-by-prompt visibility scores and trends
  • Spot competitor overlap and source-page opportunities
  • Turn findings into content and SEO action items
